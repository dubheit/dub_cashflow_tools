DUB Treasury Base

Complete treasury management with accounting integration

Overview

DUB Treasury Base provides comprehensive treasury management for Odoo, including treasury accounts, credit lines, financial instruments, and full accounting integration. Designed for Italian companies but adaptable to international standards.

💳 Treasury Accounts

Manage bank accounts, cash, and internal accounts with real-time balance tracking.

💰 Credit Lines

Track overdrafts, invoice advances, factoring, and other credit facilities with usage monitoring.

📈 Financial Instruments

Manage loans, mortgages, leasing, bonds, and partner loans with automatic installment generation.

📖 Accounting Integration

Automatic journal entry generation for disbursements, payments, and reclassifications.

Financial Instruments

Manage various types of financial instruments with support for both inbound (financing received) and outbound (financing granted) flows.

Supported Instrument Types

Type Italian Term Description
Bank Loan Finanziamento Standard bank loans with periodic repayments
Mortgage Mutuo Long-term secured loans, typically with real estate collateral
Leasing Leasing Equipment or vehicle leasing contracts
Bond Obbligazione Corporate or partner bonds
Partner Loan Prestito soci Loans to/from company partners
Factoring Factoring Factoring agreements

Amortization Methods

  • French (Constant installment): Equal periodic payments, interest decreases over time
  • Italian (Constant principal): Equal principal portions, decreasing total payments
  • Bullet: Single payment at maturity
  • Custom: Manual entry of installment schedule

Accounting Integration

The module generates journal entries automatically, ensuring accurate accounting records for all treasury operations.

Financial Instrument Entries

Disbursement Entry (Inbound Loan)

Generated when activating a loan received from a bank:

Debit: Bank Account ................ 100,000.00
Credit: Long-term Liability ........ 100,000.00

Installment Payment Entry

Generated for each installment payment:

Debit: Long-term Liability .......... 8,000.00
Debit: Interest Expense ............. 2,000.00
Credit: Short-term Liability ....... 10,000.00

Credit Line Entries

Utilization (Drawing)

When drawing funds from a credit line:

Debit: Bank Account ................ 50,000.00
Credit: Credit Line Liability ...... 50,000.00

Repayment

When repaying a credit line:

Debit: Credit Line Liability ....... 50,000.00
Credit: Bank Account ............... 50,000.00

Monitor compliance with bank covenants directly from the credit line, including common ratios:

  • Debt/Equity Ratio
  • Current Ratio
  • Interest Coverage Ratio
  • Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR)
  • Leverage Ratio

Configuration

Initial Setup

  1. Create Treasury Accounts: Define your bank accounts and cash accounts
  2. Configure Credit Lines: Set up credit facilities with limits and conditions
  3. Set Accounting Accounts: Configure the general ledger accounts for each instrument type
  4. Create Financial Instruments: Enter your loans, mortgages, and other instruments
  5. Generate Installments: Automatically create the payment schedule

Accounting Configuration

For each financial instrument, configure:

  • Journal: The accounting journal for entries
  • Long-term Account: Liability (inbound) or Asset (outbound) for maturities > 12 months
  • Short-term Account: Liability (inbound) or Asset (outbound) for maturities ≤ 12 months
  • Interest Account: Interest expense (inbound) or income (outbound)
  • Disbursement Account: Bank account for loan disbursement
Note: Long-term/Short-term reclassification helps maintain accurate balance sheet presentation by moving upcoming principal payments to current liabilities.

Integration

DUB Treasury Base integrates seamlessly with:

  • Odoo Accounting: Full integration with journal entries and bank reconciliation
  • DUB Cashflow: Treasury data feeds into cash flow forecasting
  • Vendor Bills: Link vendor invoices to financial instruments for tracking
